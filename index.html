    How to use it

    1. Point the camera at the code, or pick a screenshot of one from Messenger or Viber.
    2. Read the site name in large letters and the checks under it.
    3. Open the link if it matches what you expected. Close it if it shows red.

    Scan with this in place of your camera app

    Your phone's camera app opens a link as soon as it reads the code. Install QR Protect to your home screen and scan from here, and use Share on a screenshot to send it to QR Protect.

    Payment codes

    A QR Ph code shows the name of the account receiving the money. Scammers put their own sticker over a store's code, so check that the name matches the store before you pay.
